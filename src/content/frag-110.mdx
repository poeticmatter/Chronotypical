Can you not look an hour ahead to not know what I'm going to do?
I could, but I may remember it anyway.
What do you mean?
When I look at any point in the future I get the memories of that version of me.
So you remember everything that is going to happen?
No, only the big things. If I want to know a small thing I need to go check.
